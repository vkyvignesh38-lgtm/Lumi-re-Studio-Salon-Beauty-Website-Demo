About Lumière

Modern Beauty & Grooming, Personalised for You.

Lumière Studio is designed around one simple idea — everyone deserves to feel confident and cared for. Our team brings together beauty, grooming and wellness services for women and men in a relaxed, personalised environment.

Our story

A studio where everyone feels at ease

Too many salons feel made for someone else. Lumière was imagined as the opposite: a single, welcoming space where a bride, a busy professional and someone who just wants a good haircut all feel equally at home.

The studio pairs a bright styling floor with a quieter grooming lounge and a softly lit spa room. Services are planned around you — your hair type, your skin, your schedule and the look you actually want to wear every day.

There's no rush and no upselling. Just thoughtful consultations, clean and careful work, and a calm hour that's entirely yours.

How a visit works

Simple, thoughtful, unhurried

    We start with a short chat about your hair, skin or grooming goals and any sensitivities.

  2. Personalise

    Your specialist recommends a service, products and timing that suit you — never a one-size-fits-all menu.
