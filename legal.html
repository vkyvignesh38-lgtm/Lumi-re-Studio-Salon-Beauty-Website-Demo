Transparency about what this website is — and isn't.

Demo Project Notice

Lumière Studio is a fictional brand created for portfolio demonstration purposes.

What this means

  • Lumière Studio is not a real salon, and no services can be purchased here.
  • The appointment form is a demo booking only — no real appointment is ever scheduled, and form data never leaves your browser.
  • All prices are demo pricing shown for portfolio purposes.
  • Team members (Aaradhya, Arjun, Priya and Meera) are fictional profiles. No real employment, certifications, awards or years of experience are claimed.
  • There are no real customers, reviews, awards, certifications or business results on this website.
  • Contact details are placeholders: “+91 XXXXX XXXXX”, the .demo email address and the Chennai location do not belong to a real business. The map is an illustration.

Privacy

This demo has no backend, analytics or cookies. Information typed into the appointment form is only used in your browser to build the on-screen summary and the optional WhatsApp / email links, which you choose whether to send.
